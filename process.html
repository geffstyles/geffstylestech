How we work

A transparent process so you always know what happens next, what we need from you, and what you receive.

01

Discovery & consultation

Free call or WhatsApp chat to understand your goals, audience, must-have features, timeline and budget range. You leave with clarity on whether we are a fit and what the next step looks like.

You provide: goals, examples you like, any existing branding/content.
You get: recommended approach and rough timeline.

02

Proposal & agreement

A written proposal covering scope, deliverables, milestones, pricing and payment schedule. No work starts until you approve and the deposit is received.

You provide: feedback on the proposal, signed agreement if required.
You get: fixed or phased pricing and a clear project plan.

03

Design

Wireframes and visual design aligned with your brand. You review and request changes within the agreed revision rounds before development begins.

You provide: logo, colours, content drafts, timely feedback.
You get: approved design direction ready for build.

04

Development

Clean, tested implementation. You receive progress updates and staging links where useful so you can see the product take shape.

You provide: final content, images, product data as needed.
You get: a working product on a staging environment for review.

05

Launch

Final checks, deployment to your domain/hosting, and basic training so your team can manage everyday updates.

You provide: domain/hosting access if applicable, final sign-off.
You get: live product and handover documentation.

06

Support

A post-launch support window is included with every package. Optional maintenance plans cover updates, security and small improvements ongoing.

You get: defined support period plus optional retainers for peace of mind.
