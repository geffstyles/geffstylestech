How much does a website cost in Nigeria in 2026?

Updated 20268 min read

Website prices in Nigeria vary widely. A simple one-page site is not the same as a full online store with payments and inventory. This guide explains typical ranges and what actually affects cost.

Typical price ranges

  • Starter / brochure site: from around ₦100k–₦200k for a focused multi-page presence
  • Business website: often ₦150k–₦400k depending on pages, design depth and features
  • E-commerce store: commonly from ₦600k upward once products, payments and admin tools are included
  • Custom web apps: project-based; scoped after discovery

At GEFFSTYLES WEB SAT, published starting prices are: business websites from ₦150k, e-commerce from ₦600k, and mobile apps from ₦1M — always confirmed in a written proposal.

What drives the price up

  • Number of pages and custom sections
  • E-commerce, bookings or user accounts
  • Payment gateways and logistics integrations
  • Custom design vs template-based work
  • Content writing, photography and SEO depth
  • Tight deadlines or complex revisions

What you should budget beyond “the website”

Domain, hosting, SSL, payment fees and occasional maintenance are separate from build cost. Plan for them so the site stays online and secure after launch.

How to get an accurate quote

Share your goals, examples you like, must-have features and timeline. A short discovery call is enough to produce a clear proposal — not a vague “it depends” answer.
